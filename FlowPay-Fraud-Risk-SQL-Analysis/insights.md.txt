# 📊 FlowPay Fraud & Revenue Risk Analysis — Key Insights

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes payment transactions, fraud behavior, refunds, operational failures, and user risk patterns within a simulated fintech payment ecosystem.

The analysis combines:

* Fraud analytics
* Revenue leakage analysis
* Behavioral segmentation
* Velocity detection
* Retry recovery analytics
* Geographic anomaly detection
* Risk scoring

The goal was to identify:

* major revenue leakage drivers,
* operational inefficiencies,
* fraud attack patterns,
* and high-risk user behaviors.

---

# 💰 Revenue & Payment Funnel Insights

## Platform-Level Revenue Flow

| Metric                  | Value    |
| ----------------------- | -------- |
| Total Attempted Volume  | ₹5.65B   |
| Successful Payments     | ₹4.95B   |
| Failed Payments         | ₹562.13M |
| Fraudulent Transactions | ₹136.27M |
| Refund Amount           | ₹109.86M |
| Net Revenue Retained    | ₹4.84B   |
| Net Revenue Retention   | 85.69%   |

## Key Findings

* Nearly **14.31% of transaction value leaked** due to failures, fraud, and refunds.
* Failed transactions contributed the largest revenue loss component.
* Fraud and refund leakage were materially smaller than operational payment failures.

## Business Insight

Operational payment reliability has a larger financial impact than direct fraud losses.

---

# 💳 Payment Method Insights

## UPI Dominates Platform Revenue

UPI generated the highest transaction volume and retained revenue across all payment methods.

## Card Payments Showed Higher Failure Rates

Credit Card and Debit Card payments experienced the highest authorization failure rates (~10.8%).

## Key Findings

* UPI carried elevated fraud exposure relative to cards.
* Wallets showed relatively stable operational performance.
* Card-based payment flows require optimization.

## Business Insight

Improving card authorization and payment gateway routing can significantly reduce revenue leakage.

---

# 🏪 Merchant Category Insights

## Highest Revenue Categories

Top-performing categories included:

* Electronics
* Fuel
* Food & Dining
* Education
* Telecom

## Highest Risk Categories

### Gaming

* Highest fraud rate
* Highest refund rate

### Real Estate

* Elevated refund exposure

### Utilities & Grocery

* Higher payment failure rates

## Business Insight

Certain merchant categories generate strong revenue but carry elevated operational and fraud risk.

---

# 👤 User Segment Insights

## Normal Users Drive Platform Revenue

The majority of revenue and transactions came from standard user segments.

## High-Value Low-Activity Users

This segment demonstrated:

* lowest fraud rates,
* lowest failure rates,
* highest revenue retention.

## High-Activity Low-Value Users

This segment showed:

* highest operational failure rates,
* lower revenue efficiency,
* elevated behavioral risk.

## Business Insight

High transaction frequency without proportional revenue contribution is a strong operational risk signal.

---

# 📱 Multi-Device Behavior Insights

## Device Usage Distribution

* ~90% of users operated on 1–2 devices.
* Only a small segment used 4+ devices.

## Key Findings

* Fraud rates remained relatively stable across device groups.
* Device count alone was not a strong standalone fraud predictor.

## Business Insight

Device behavior becomes more valuable when combined with velocity and geographic signals.

---

# 🌍 Geographic Movement Insights

## Impossible Travel Detection

Only a very small number of users exhibited suspicious city-switching behavior within unrealistic time windows.

## Key Findings

* Impossible travel behavior is rare but highly suspicious.
* Most users demonstrated normal geographic movement patterns.

## Business Insight

Geographic anomaly detection provides a high-confidence fraud signal.

---

# ⚡ Velocity Analysis Insights

## Transaction Burst Behavior

Medium-velocity users:

* contributed a significant share of transactions,
* showed materially higher failure rates.

## Key Findings

* Rapid transaction bursts correlate strongly with operational instability.
* High-speed activity is associated with elevated payment risk.

## Business Insight

Velocity monitoring is important for both fraud prevention and payment stability.

---

# 🔁 Retry Behavior Insights

## Retry Recovery Performance

Retry systems recovered approximately ₹140.58M in otherwise failed transaction value.

## Key Findings

* Retry recovery rate was approximately 25%.
* Nearly 75% of failed transaction value remained unrecovered.

## Business Insight

Retry optimization represents a major revenue recovery opportunity.

---

# 🚨 Fraud Pattern Insights

## Top Fraud Categories

| Fraud Type       | Share  |
| ---------------- | ------ |
| Account Takeover | 22.55% |
| Identity Theft   | 15.82% |
| Device Spoofing  | 15.58% |
| Card Not Present | 15.53% |

## Key Findings

* Authentication and identity-related fraud dominate the ecosystem.
* Device manipulation and credential compromise are major attack vectors.

## Business Insight

Account security and device intelligence systems should be prioritized.

---

# 🔄 Refund Insights

## Top Refund Drivers

| Refund Reason         | Share  |
| --------------------- | ------ |
| Wrong Amount Charged  | 22.64% |
| Duplicate Transaction | 19.10% |
| Merchant Error        | 14.01% |

## Key Findings

* Billing inconsistencies and duplicate transactions are major operational issues.
* Fulfillment and merchant-quality problems contribute significantly to refunds.

## Business Insight

Operational quality improvements can directly reduce post-payment revenue leakage.

---

# 🧠 Risk Scoring Model Insights

## Final Risk Segmentation

| Risk Level     | Users  |
| -------------- | ------ |
| 🚨 High Risk   | 3,573  |
| 🟡 Medium Risk | 11,060 |
| 🟢 Low Risk    | 10,367 |

## Key Findings

* High-risk users showed:

  * elevated fraud rates,
  * elevated failure rates,
  * strong association with reported fraud.

* Low-risk users demonstrated stable transaction behavior with minimal fraud exposure.

## Business Insight

Behavior-based risk scoring significantly improves user risk differentiation.

---

# 🎯 Strategic Conclusions

## 1. Payment Failures Are the Largest Revenue Leakage Source

Operational inefficiencies contribute more revenue loss than direct fraud.

---

## 2. Fraud Is Primarily Identity & Authentication Driven

Account takeover, identity theft, and device spoofing dominate fraud activity.

---

## 3. Retry Systems Provide Meaningful Revenue Recovery

Improved retry orchestration can recover substantial transaction value.

---

## 4. Behavioral Signals Improve Fraud Detection

Velocity, retries, device usage, and geographic movement provide strong fraud intelligence signals.

---

## 5. Risk Scoring Is More Effective Than Static Rules

Combining:

* transaction behavior,
* retries,
* velocity,
* device patterns,
* city movement,
* and user value

creates a stronger fraud detection framework.
